Ever been to Lestat’s and seen the chalkboard? Every week there’s a new topic, and customers pick up the chalk and write their answers. You read what the person before you wrote, you add yours, and for a minute a room full of strangers is having the same conversation.

This is our version of that board, and it only works if a lot of different people write on it.

The question on our board: what song gets you through the hard part of the day? The one for the drive in when you already know it’s going to be a long day. The one that resets you in the four minutes between third period and lunch. Every answer goes up next to everyone else’s.
