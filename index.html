Not another newsletter

Built to be used, not filed away

Every issue is a real interactive page, not a PDF. Nothing here needs an account, and nothing typed into it goes anywhere but your own device.

A different shape every month

September is a bell schedule. Other issues take their own structural concept — a shelf of envelopes, a quilt you fill in yourself. No two feel the same.

Tools you actually use

A breathing timer, a mile logger, a recipe you can print. Everything saves locally as you go — open it again next week and it remembers where you left off.

Private by default

No accounts, no analytics, nothing reported to the committee or the district. Whatever you save stays on the device you saved it on.
