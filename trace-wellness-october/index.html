NO. 01 Front desk

Nobody warns you about October

September has adrenaline. December has a countdown. October has neither, and that is exactly why it is the month people quietly run out of gas.

The back-to-school push is over. The routines are set, the novelty has worn off, and the calendar between here and Thanksgiving is a solid wall of instructional days. Add conferences, a testing window, and the first real wave of parent email, and you get the month that most quietly takes people apart.

So this issue is not a pep talk. It is a set of tools you open at the moment you need one — a shelf of envelopes, an honest read on where your energy is actually going, and a place to put the worry that is following you to the parking lot.

Everything on this page saves to your own device and nowhere else. No accounts, no tracking, nothing reported to anyone.

October is also

  • Breast Cancer Awareness Month — a nudge to book the screening you have been putting off.
  • National Bullying Prevention Month — Unity Day is Wednesday, October 21.
  • World Mental Health Day — Saturday, October 10.
  • Domestic Violence Awareness Month.
  • National Physical Therapy Month — if something has hurt since August, that is not normal.

    NO. 04 Eight minutes

    The worry window

    Worry expands to fill whatever space you give it. So give it eight minutes and a hard edge. Type everything that is chewing on you. When the window closes, it clears — and you answer one question.

    08:00

    This text is never saved — not to this device, not anywhere. Closing the window erases it on purpose.

    Window closed

    Now the only question that matters

    Of everything you just wrote down, which parts are actually yours to carry? Some of it belongs to an administrator, a family, a system, or next month. Say the ones that are yours out loud, and put the rest down until Monday.

    If the same worry shows up in this window three weeks running, that is not a worry anymore. That is a problem, and it deserves a person — a colleague, your union rep, or a counselor through VEBA.

    NO. 05 Walktober

    Walktober

    The challenge runs October 1 through October 31. Free to enter, no fitness level assumed, and steps are not the only thing that counts.

    The challenge

    A month of movement, a little friendly competition, and not much else. Steps count, and so does anything else that gets you moving. Think of it as permission to take the long way in.

    Runs October 1 through October 31. Sign-ups are open.
    Register for Walktober

    Your own quiet count

    0.0 miles of 40

    Just for you — kept on this device, not reported anywhere. Nobody is checking your number but you.

    October is good for walking

    The heat finally breaks, the light goes gold around five, and the tourists have gone home. Three that are worth the drive:

    • Sunset Cliffs at golden hour. Flat, free, and it does not feel like exercise.
    • Lake Murray for a straight, shadeless, no-decisions loop.
    • Los Peñasquitos Canyon to the falls if you have a whole morning.

    And if a walk is not happening this month: October is Physical Therapy Month. Whatever has been aching since August is worth one appointment.
