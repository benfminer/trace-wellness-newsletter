We’re building one playlist, together — and it only works if it’s actually yours.

Everybody has one. The song you put on for the drive in when you already know it’s going to be a long day. The one that resets you in the four minutes between third period and lunch.

We want it. All of them, actually — one big board of what gets this staff through the hard part of the day.
