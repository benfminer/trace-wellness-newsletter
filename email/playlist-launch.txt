THE WELLNESS CHAMPION PLAYLIST
TRACE Schools - Wellness Committee

We're building one playlist, together - and it only works if it's
actually yours.

Everybody has one. The song you put on for the drive in when you
already know it's going to be a long day. The one that resets you in
the four minutes between third period and lunch.

We want it. All of them, actually - one big board of what gets this
staff through the hard part of the day.

HERE'S THE FUN PART
Repeats are encouraged. The more people send the same song, the bigger
it gets on the board. That's the whole game.

WHAT THE FORM ASKS
  01  The song
  02  The artist
  03  Your name
  04  One line on what it does for you - this is the part people
      actually read
